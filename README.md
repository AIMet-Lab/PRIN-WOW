# About the Project
The WOW project focuses on developing an ontology-based archive that collects and semantically structures data about the writings of Italian actresses, known as “Divagrafie.” These writings explore the intersections between:
- Writing: Personal narratives and creative works.
- Acting Performance: Artistic expression and its relationship to text.
- Star Image Construction: How writings contribute to the public and cultural image of actresses.
By analysing these dynamics, the WOW archive bridges autobiographical materials, cinema studies, and semantic technologies, offering a unique perspective on the cultural and artistic contributions of Italian actresses.

# Goals
- Data Collection: Aggregate autobiographical writings by Italian actresses into a structured archive.
- Ontology Development: Create an ontology to semantically describe relationships between texts, performances, and star imagery.
- Knowledge Graph (KG) and Linked Data: Enable researchers to explore and query data about the dynamics between writing and performance through a KG technologies, ensuring interoperability with broader digital humanities resources.
- Preservation: Digitally preserve a significant but underexplored area of cultural heritage.
